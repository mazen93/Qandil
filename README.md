## 🌟 About Qandil (عن قنديل)

**Qandil (قنديل)** is a modern, beautifully crafted Islamic companion application built for iOS and Android. Designed with an unwavering commitment to **privacy**, **offline capability**, and **spiritual peace**, Qandil provides a calm and ad-free environment for your daily worship, Quranic reading, and remembrance.

---

## ✨ Key Features (المميزات الرئيسية)

- **📖 Authentic Uthmanic Quran Reader:**
  - High-clarity Uthmanic text (Hafs an Asim) with custom typography and eye-friendly reading themes.
  - Authentic Tafsir (Ibn Kathir, Al-Muyassar) and multi-language translations (English, French, etc.).
  - Distraction-free reading with verse memorization practice (progressive word-masking mode).
  - Quick surah and ayah search, bookmarks, and last-read recovery.

- **🎧 Audio Recitations & Offline Playback:**
  - High-fidelity verse-by-verse and full-surah recitations from renowned reciters (Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Abdulbasit Abdulsamad, and more).
  - Background audio playback with lock screen media controls.
  - Download surahs for 100% offline listening anywhere.

- **🕌 Accurate Prayer Times & Full Adhan:**
  - 100% on-device astronomical prayer calculations that work worldwide without needing internet.
  - Supports all major calculation conventions: Egyptian General Authority of Survey, Umm al-Qura (Makkah), Muslim World League, University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, and ISNA.
  - Customizable alerts: full Adhan audio notifications or gentle alerts with customizable reminder offsets.
  - Real-time precision Qibla compass with Kaaba bearing angle.

- **📿 Tactile 3D Tasbih & Daily Adhkar:**
  - Realistic 3D 33-bead medallion Tasbih counter with haptic feedback and fluid animations.
  - Presets for popular Adhkar (SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, Astaghfirullah) and custom dhikr targets.
  - Comprehensive bundled morning and evening Adhkar (أذكار الصباح والمساء) with interactive tap counters.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security (الخصوصية والأمان)

- **No Ads:** Absolutely zero commercial advertising, banners, or tracking.
- **No User Accounts:** No sign-up, email, phone number, or social logins required.
- **On-Device Data:** All bookmarks, reading history, and Tasbih tallies are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database.
- **Location Privacy:** Location permissions are used strictly on-device to compute astronomical prayer times and Qibla direction. Location data is never sent to any server.
