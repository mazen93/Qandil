## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Why are prayer times slightly different from my local mosque?
Check your calculation convention under **Settings → Prayer Calculation Method**. Different authorities (Egyptian General Authority of Survey, Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Karachi, ISNA) calculate Fajr and Isha angles slightly differently. Set the method recommended by your local ministry of Islamic affairs.

### 2. Why did the Adhan sound silent or only show a banner?
Ensure:
- On iOS: Go to **Settings → Qandil → Notifications** and ensure **Sounds**, **Banners**, and **Time-Sensitive Notifications** are turned **ON**. Also ensure your device's physical mute switch / Focus mode allows time-sensitive alerts.
- On Android: Ensure battery optimization is set to "Unrestricted" or "Not optimized" for Qandil so Android does not terminate background alarm schedules.

### 3. Does Qandil work offline?
Yes! All text Mushaf, prayer time calculations, Qibla compass, Adhkar, and Tasbih work 100% offline. You can also download audio recitations for any Surah to listen without an internet connection.
